Frequently asked

Common questions, honest answers.

Will this work for my niche?

Probably yes if your app's audience exists on TikTok or Instagram. We see the strongest results in: dating/relationships, fitness, mental health, productivity, parenting, journaling, finance, and education.

How do you measure CPI when SKAN is broken?

Per-creator promo codes. Every creator gets a unique code displayed in their content. Code redemptions = clean attribution. It undercounts somewhat (some users skip the code) but the relative signal between creators is reliable.

Do I have to commit long-term?

No. Monthly billing, cancel anytime. The founder pricing locks in the rate — not your commitment.

What if creators flake?

Industry flake rate is ~30%. We pay creators on post (not upfront), so flakes don't cost you money. If a creator no-shows, we replace them in the next batch.

Why a $99 deposit?

Filters out tire-kickers and saves both of us time. Fully refundable. Applied to your first month if you sign on.

How is this different from Modash, Insense, or Aspire?

Modash sells data — you still do all the work. Insense is a marketplace where creators apply (you don't pick). Aspire is enterprise ($2–5k/mo, sales-led). ReachShore is a managed service for indies, end-to-end, $499/mo founder rate.
